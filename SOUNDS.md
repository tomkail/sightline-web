# Sound list

Every sound the game needs, with a prompt to paste into the ElevenLabs sound effects generator.

## How to use this list

- **Style for everything:** a paparazzi scrum heard through a cartoon. Camera mechanics, flashbulbs, paper and a rowdy press pack. Dry, close, punchy, no reverb, no music bed unless stated.
- **Length:** each entry gives the length the game needs. ElevenLabs tends not to go much under half a second, so generate at the shortest setting and trim the silence off the end.
- **Start:** every sound must begin instantly, with no fade-in or lead-in silence, or it will feel late on a button press.
- **Format:** mono, exported as MP3 or OGG. Loud and consistent; the game sets the volume.
- **Take several:** generate three or four of each and keep the best. For the ones marked *variations*, keep two or three different takes so repeats don't sound identical.
- **Player pitch:** today every player has their own pitch for the warning beep, so the room can hear who is in trouble. The four sounds marked *pitched per player* will be sped up or slowed down by the game to do the same, so they need a clear single pitch and no background.
