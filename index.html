How to play

  1. Put the target on a table.Draw it, print it, or show it on a spare phone.
  2. Point your camera at it.Your phone chimes and shows a green ring when it sees the target.
  3. Stop the others seeing it.Block their camera, cover the target, move it, stand in the way.
  4. Lose sight and your phone beeps.Your bar drains until you find the target again. Empty bar: you're out.
  5. Last phone still seeing it wins.

Hold the short edge of a bank card against the black bar. Tap until the bar is exactly as long as the card (5.4 cm).
