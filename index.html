Sightline

Keep the target in view. Block everyone else's. Last camera still seeing it wins.

Starting camera…

This is what your phone sees. Point it at the target: a green ring means it has found it, and that is all you need to do in the game.

🔊 Sound on, volume up. Your phone beeps when you're losing sight, so the others can hear who is in trouble. On iPhone, flip the silent switch off.

Make the target: on any paper, draw a thick closed ring as big as the page (stroke about a finger wide), then a solid filled dot in the middle, about a third of the ring's width. Leave white space between them. Go over the ring twice with a marker pen so it has no gaps. No paper? Lay a spare phone flat showing the target screen.

Distance settings

Distance comes from how big the target looks to your camera. For better accuracy, hold the phone exactly 50 cm from the target in the game screen and tap "Calibrate".
